About MahjongCenter

MahjongCenter exists to make American Mahjong easier to understand.

American Mahjong is a wonderful game with a steep first step. New players are handed a card full of colored numbers, a pile of tiles and a stream of unfamiliar words, often all at once. We write the explanations we wish we'd had: plain English, straight to the answer, and honest about where tables differ.

What you'll find here

  • Beginner guides to how the game works
  • Rules explanations for the questions that come up mid-game
  • Strategy, starting with the Charleston
  • Community guides for teaching, hosting and finding a game
  • Gear guides to help you buy the right set the first time

We're independent

MahjongCenter is an independent educational resource. We are not affiliated with or endorsed by the National Mah Jongg League, and we don't speak for it.

We don't reproduce the League's annual card or list its hands. The card is the League's work, and players need their own current copy to play. You can get it from the National Mah Jongg League. For formal rules, official League materials are the authority; for tournaments, so are the event's own rules.

How we work

Our articles are written and reviewed by the MahjongCenter Editorial Team. Read our editorial policy to see how we check rules content, handle time-sensitive product information and correct mistakes.

How the site is funded

MahjongCenter may show advertising and may earn commissions from some product links. Our affiliate disclosure explains how that works. Neither affects what we write about the rules.
