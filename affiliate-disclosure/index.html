Affiliate disclosure

MahjongCenter may earn a commission when readers purchase through some product links. This does not increase the reader's price.

How it works

Some links in our gear guides may be affiliate links. If you click one and buy something, the retailer may pay us a small commission. You pay the same price you would otherwise.

Pages that contain affiliate links carry a short disclosure near the top.

What it doesn't change

  • Affiliate relationships don't decide what we recommend or how we describe a product.
  • Our rules, strategy and community guides are never shaped by affiliate relationships.
  • We don't claim hands-on testing unless we've done it, and we don't invent ratings or reviews.

Why we use them

Commissions and advertising help cover the cost of running MahjongCenter and keep every guide free to read.
