Editorial policy

How we write, check and update our guides.

Our goal

Every guide should help a real player at a real table. We aim for clear, accurate explanations in plain English, and we'd rather say "house rules vary" than claim certainty we don't have.

Accuracy and review

  • Educational articles are reviewed for clarity and accuracy before publication.
  • Rules articles are checked against reliable, current sources. Where home games, tournaments or official materials differ, we say so.
  • We distinguish American Mahjong from other variants and label other variants clearly.
  • We don't reproduce the National Mah Jongg League's annual card or its hands.

Time-sensitive information

Product details, prices and online services change often. We review this kind of information before publishing and mark it for regular re-checking. When we can't confirm a current price, we leave it out rather than guess. Always confirm details with the seller or service before you buy.

Product recommendations

  • We only describe hands-on testing when we've actually done it. Otherwise, we say we compared specifications, materials, included accessories and player-oriented features.
  • We don't publish invented scores, star ratings, review counts or testimonials.
  • Affiliate relationships do not determine our editorial conclusions. See our affiliate disclosure.

Authorship

Articles are published by the MahjongCenter Editorial Team. We don't invent authors or credentials.

Corrections

If you think something is wrong, unclear or out of date, please tell us through our contact page. Include the page address and, if you can, a source. We review every correction request and update pages when needed.
