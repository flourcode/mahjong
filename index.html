Common questions

  • Can you use a Joker in a pair?No. Jokers only work in groups of three or more.
  • How many tiles do you start with?14 for East, the dealer. 13 for everyone else.
  • What makes a hand dead?Usually a wrong tile count, an impossible exposure or an illegal Joker.
  • What does “Soap” mean?The White Dragon, which also counts as zero.
  • What is a blind pass?Passing on incoming tiles unseen on the last pass of a Charleston.
  • How do you exchange a Joker?On your turn, give the real tile and take the Joker.
