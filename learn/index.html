Learn American Mahjong, one step at a time

You don't need to learn everything before your first game. Learn things in this order and each piece will make the next one easier.

  1. Get the big picture

    Start with the overall flow of a hand: deal, Charleston, draw and discard, call tiles, declare Mahjong. Our main beginner guide covers all of it without drowning you in edge cases.

    How to Play American Mahjong: A Beginner's Guide

  2. Learn the words people use at the table

    Pung, Kong, Soap, blind pass, concealed. Once you know about twenty terms, conversations at the table stop sounding like code.

    American Mahjong Glossary

  3. Understand the card

    The annual card is how American Mahjong decides what counts as a winning hand. Learn what the colors, numbers, letters, X and C mean before you try to memorize any hands.

    How to read the NMJL card

  4. Get comfortable with the Charleston

    The tile-passing phase at the start of every hand is where beginners feel the most pressure. Learn the order of passes and a few simple habits.

    How the Charleston works

  5. Know the Joker rules

    Jokers are powerful, and misusing one is the fastest way to a dead hand. The rules are short once you see them laid out.

    Joker rules and exchanges

  6. Set up a table yourself

    Washing the tiles, building the walls, breaking the wall and dealing. You'll be doing this every hand, so it's worth knowing.

    How to set up an American Mahjong game

Playing with Chinese or other rules?

MahjongCenter focuses on American Mahjong played with the National Mah Jongg League card. If you learned a different style first, read how Chinese and American Mahjong differ before your first American game. The differences are bigger than most people expect.

Learning with friends?

If someone at your table is teaching, share our guide to teaching a beginner with them. It explains the open-hand approach that makes a first game much less stressful.
