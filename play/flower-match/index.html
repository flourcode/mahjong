How to play

Every board has exactly one pair of identical flowers. Everything else is one of a kind. Tap a flower, then tap the one you think matches it. Find the pair and a fresh board appears. There's no timer and no penalty for a wrong guess.

Stuck? Hints are free: the first hint highlights one tile of the pair, and the second shows both. Change the board size any time for a quicker or more challenging round. You can also use the Tab key to move between tiles and Enter or Space to choose one.

Is this Mahjong?

No. Flower Match borrows the look and feel of Mahjong tiles, but it's a simple matching game. American Mahjong is a four-player game built around the Charleston, Jokers and an annual card of winning hands. If you'd like to learn it, start with our beginner's guide.
