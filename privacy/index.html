Privacy policy

Last updated September 2026. This policy explains, in plain language, what information is collected when you use MahjongCenter and what choices you have.

Who we are

MahjongCenter (mahjongcenter.com) is operated by [SITE OWNER NAME]. You can contact us at [CONTACT EMAIL].

Information we collect

We don't require accounts or sign-ups. You can read every guide and play Flower Match without giving us personal information.

  • Server logs. Like most websites, our hosting provider may automatically record technical information such as your IP address, browser type, pages requested and the date and time of a visit, for security and to keep the site running.
  • Messages you send us. If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you include. We use it to reply and don't sell it.
  • Site search. Searches on this site run entirely in your browser. We don't record what you search for.
  • Flower Match. The game runs in your browser. It doesn't send your play to us.

Analytics [IF ENABLED]

We may use an analytics service, such as Google Analytics, to understand how visitors use the site, for example which pages are read most. These services use cookies or similar technologies to collect information such as pages visited, time on page, device type and approximate location. We use this information in aggregate to improve the site.

Advertising [IF ENABLED]

We may show ads served by Google AdSense and other third-party advertising providers.

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this site and/or other sites on the internet.
  • You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google's Ads Settings. You can also learn about opting out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalized advertising at www.aboutads.info.
  • To learn how Google uses information from sites that use its services, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

Where the law requires your consent before advertising or analytics cookies are used, such as in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, we will use a consent management platform certified by Google to ask for your choices before those cookies are set. You'll be able to change your choices at any time.
