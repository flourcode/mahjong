Quick answers

Can a Joker be used in a pair?
No. Jokers only work in groups of three or more identical tiles. Details
How many tiles does each player start with?
East, the dealer, starts with 14. Everyone else starts with 13. How the deal works
What makes a hand dead?
Usually a structural mistake: the wrong number of tiles, an exposure that can't match any hand on the card, or an illegal Joker. All five causes
What does "Soap" mean?
It's the nickname for the White Dragon, which also counts as zero in year hands. Glossary entry
What does C mean on the card?
Concealed. You can't call discards for that hand except the tile that wins it. Exposed vs. concealed

Where do the rules come from?

American Mahjong as most people play it follows the National Mah Jongg League, which publishes a new card of winning hands every year along with official rules. MahjongCenter is independent and doesn't reproduce the card. We explain how the game works so the card makes sense when you hold it. Home games often add house rules, so it's always worth asking what your table does.
