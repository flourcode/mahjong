How to Use and Exchange Jokers in American Mahjong

Jokers can substitute for tiles in groups of three or more, but they can't be used for singles or pairs. On your turn, you can swap a real tile for someone's exposed Joker.

Updated September 2026 / 4 min read / MahjongCenter Editorial Team

Quick answer: Jokers only work in groups of three or more identical tiles. A Joker can never complete a pair or a single tile, and it can never be passed in the Charleston.

A standard American Mahjong set has 8 Jokers. Because the card often asks for five or six of the same tile, and there are only four of any real tile, Jokers make many hands possible. They're also where a lot of beginner mistakes happen. This guide is part of our American Mahjong rules collection.

Where Jokers work

Jokers can stand in for any tile in a group of three or more identical tiles:

  • Pung (3): for example, two 4 Dots and a Joker
  • Kong (4): for example, three Norths and a Joker
  • Quint (5): always needs at least one Joker
  • Sextet (6): always needs at least two Jokers

A group can even be made entirely of Jokers, as long as it's a legal group size for the hand and you're clear about which tile the group represents.

Where Jokers never work

  • Pairs. A pair must be two real, identical tiles, even if it's the last thing you need to win.
  • Single tiles. Singles in a hand, like individual numbers in a year or one of each Wind, must be real tiles.
  • The Charleston. You can't pass a Joker. If you're dealt one, it stays with you.

Watch out: Exposing a pair or single that uses a Joker is an illegal exposure. It can make your hand dead.

How to exchange a Joker

If a player has an exposed group on top of their rack that includes a Joker, and you hold the real tile that Joker represents, you can take the Joker. Players call this an exchange or redeeming a Joker.

  1. Wait for your turn. You can't exchange out of turn.
  2. Start your turn normally by drawing from the wall (or by calling a discard).
  3. Give them the real tile. If their exposed Kong is three 5 Bams and a Joker, hand them a 5 Bam. It replaces the Joker in their group.
  4. Take the Joker into your hand.
  5. Discard to end your turn, as usual.

You can make more than one exchange in a turn if you have the right tiles, and you can exchange from anyone's exposures, including your own.

?

Beginner tip: Before you discard, glance at every exposed Joker on the table. It's easy to discard the exact tile you could have traded for a Joker.

Discarding a Joker

You're allowed to discard a Joker. It sometimes happens late in a hand when all you need is a pair, which a Joker can't help with. Under common NMJL-style play, a discarded Joker can't be called by anyone. Many players think twice before throwing one, since it could have helped an opponent had they been holding it.

Jokers and dead hands

If a player's hand is declared dead, what happens to their exposed Jokers depends on how the hand died:

  • Legal exposures made earlier stay on the rack, and other players can generally still exchange for Jokers in them.
  • The illegal exposure that killed the hand is generally taken back into the player's rack, so its Jokers can't be exchanged.
  • Exposures made for a concealed hand are generally taken back too.

These are edge cases, and groups sometimes handle them differently. Agree at your table, or follow official NMJL materials for formal play. More in our dead hand guide.

The missed exchange

If you discard a tile you could have used to exchange for a Joker, the chance is gone. Once the tile is discarded, you can't take it back to make the swap. Another player may still be able to call that discard normally.

Joker questions

Can you use a Joker to complete a pair?

No. Pairs must be two real tiles. This includes the final pair you need to declare Mahjong.

Can you pass a Joker during the Charleston?

No. Jokers can't be passed in any part of the Charleston, including the courtesy pass. See how the Charleston works.

Can you discard a Joker?

Yes. Under common NMJL-style play, nobody can call it once it's discarded.

Can you exchange multiple Jokers in one turn?

Yes, if you have the matching real tiles. Each exchange is its own swap.

How many Jokers are in an American Mahjong set?

Eight are used in play. Many sets include a couple of spare Jokers or blank tiles in case one goes missing. Set those aside before you play.

Can I exchange for a Joker from my own exposure?

Yes. If you later draw the real tile that one of your own exposed Jokers represents, you can swap it in and take the Joker back into your hand.
